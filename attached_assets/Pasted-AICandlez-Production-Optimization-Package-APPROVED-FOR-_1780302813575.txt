==================================

This is the highest-leverage issue identified.

1. Stabilization grace period after entry.

2. Spread-aware stop evaluation.

   * Prevent bid/ask spread alone from triggering exits.

3. Optional confirmation logic.

   * Require two consecutive stop-loss breaches before exit.

Requirements:

* Keep stop loss at 2%.
* Do not weaken risk management.
* Prevent false stop-outs while preserving real protection.
