JARVIS SOVEREIGNTY INITIATIVE
PHASE 1 — KNOWLEDGE ACQUISITION, MEMORY INGESTION, AND BUSINESS TAKEOVER FRAMEWORK

Strategic Objective

Jarvis is evolving from an executive assistant into the Executive Operating System for all current and future businesses.

The long-term objective is to reduce dependency on any individual platform, developer, AI provider, or hosting provider.

Jarvis must become the primary holder of operational knowledge.

Future businesses include:

* AICandlez
* MixtapePSD
* Natura AI
* Natura Holistic
* SEO Dashboard
* Future ventures

JARVIS MUST KNOW EVERYTHING NECESSARY TO OPERATE THESE BUSINESSES.

---

PHASE 1 OBJECTIVE

Create the complete framework that allows Jarvis to acquire, store, organize, retrieve, understand, and manage business knowledge.

Do not focus on one business.

Build the system that can learn any business.

EXTERNAL VAULT INTEGRATION

Design Jarvis so knowledge can be exported and backed up to:

External SSD

Jarvis Vault

Future local infrastructure

The external drive becomes the permanent knowledge archive.

Jarvis should be able to rebuild itself from the knowledge vault if necessary.

Jarvis becomes the institutional memory and executive operating system.
