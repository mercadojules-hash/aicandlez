AICANDLEZ PROFITABILITY OPTIMIZATION — PHASE 0 + CONTROLLED TEST

BACKGROUND

The profitability investigation has been completed.

Key findings:

• Risk control is working.
• Large losses have been significantly reduced.
• Average loser approximately -1.8%.
• Profit factor approximately 1.12.
• TP = 10% only triggers on approximately 6.9% of trades.
• Approximately 88.8% of winners never reach 5%.
• Average winner approximately 2.5%.
• Median winner approximately 1.3%.
• Trailing stop is rarely activated.
• Stop loss is the largest P&L drain.
• Higher confidence bands appear to perform better.
• Effective production hold time appears closer to 1 hour despite documented 6 hour max hold.

OBJECTIVE

Improve profitability without sacrificing the ability to capture larger momentum trades.

Avoid over-optimization.

Avoid killing trade frequency.

Avoid eliminating rare high-profit runners.

PHASE 0 — APPROVED

Implement immediately:

1. MFE Tracking

   * Maximum Favorable Excursion
   * Record peak unrealized profit reached
   * Timestamp when peak occurred

2. MAE Tracking

   * Maximum Adverse Excursion
   * Record worst unrealized drawdown
   * Timestamp when it occurred

3. Effective Exit Telemetry

   * Record actual TP
   * Actual SL
   * Actual trailing settings
   * Actual max hold
   * Exit reason

4. Time-to-Peak Tracking

   * Time required to reach peak profit

No behavior changes in Phase 0.

Proposed Test Profile Alpha:

SL = 2%
Confidence ≈ Current

Test:

SL = 2%
Confidence Floor = 55

Leave unchanged:

• Max Hold
• Trailing Stop
• Trailing Activation
• MTF Logic
• Risk Controls

Reasoning:

We want to improve trade quality while maintaining enough trade frequency.

A TP of 4% remains large enough to capture meaningful winners while being substantially more realistic than 10%.

A confidence floor of 55 should remove lower-quality trades while avoiding the severe trade reduction likely caused by moving directly to 60-70+.
